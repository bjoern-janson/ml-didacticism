# CAPITAL FOR MACHINES — CRITICAL APPARATUS

## Status

The reader manuscript is content-frozen. This document is the inspection layer for scope, claim ceilings, narrative provenance, cold-reader transmission, and unresolved research edges.

The apparatus does not replace the reader text and does not authorize stronger claims than the source material supports.

## 1. Reader object and evidence boundary

The reader object is:

- [Cold-reader packet](CAPITAL_FOR_MACHINES_COLD_READER_PACKET.md)

The packet is a literary and argumentative object. Its scenes are not, by themselves, experimental evidence. Understanding a scene, reconstructing its mechanism, or finding it persuasive does not establish that the underlying claim is true.

The reader text uses a continuous thought experiment and a recurring composite laboratory. Its purpose is discovery: make the reader encounter the sequence before naming the sequence. The apparatus supplies verification routes separately.

## 2. Claim ceiling

The book earns the following bounded claim:

> Inherited productive reasoning can outlive the conditions that earned its authority. When a contradiction implicates relevant inherited structure, correction can fail not because evidence is absent but because the structure cannot be legitimately reopened. That is a corrigibility limitation.

The book does not claim that:

- every reusable reason becomes defended capital;
- every benchmark is illegitimate or every evaluator is contaminated;
- representation failure explains every model failure;
- optimization over a current object set can never alter the mechanism that constructs objects;
- all authority should remain continuously contestable;
- every contradiction is sufficient to trigger reopening;
- a universal threshold for reopening has been established;
- a permanent sovereign corrector is the solution;
- records alone establish functional machine corrigibility;
- the book supplies a positive architecture for autonomous machine jurisdiction;
- the narrative events are literal reports of one laboratory or one machine.

In particular, “wrong object” means that optimization over the current object set cannot repair a failure caused by that object set itself. A mechanism change could, in principle, alter how objects are constructed or induce a new representation.

## 3. Narrative provenance

The front matter of the reader manuscript states:

> Mara and Atlas are composite narrative constructions used to carry the argument through recurring situations. Where a scene adapts an actual research event, its provenance belongs to the notes and audit material beneath the book.

The practical boundary is:

```text
narrative scene
  → illustrates a mechanism
  → may adapt research history
  → does not itself establish the generalized claim
```

Specific cautions:

- **Mara and Atlas:** composite carriers, not a claim that a single historical lab contained every described event.
- **The coin scene:** a cultural illustration of manufactured inevitability, not evidence about machine epistemology.
- **The discarded result:** dramatizes the separation between an observed output and the interpretation authorized by the experimental setup. Any literal source record belongs in the provenance audit, not in the scene alone.
- **Figure 1:** a schematic of computation and warrant relations. It is a reasoning aid, not a measurement.

Exact claim-to-artifact custody remains in the [V0.3.1 provenance audit](CAPITAL_FOR_MACHINES_V0.3.1_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md) and its [machine-readable provenance nodes](CAPITAL_FOR_MACHINES_V0.3.1_PROVENANCE_NODES.jsonl).

## 4. Cold-reader transmission record

The supplied reader reports independently recovered the same central progression:

```text
answer
  → productive reuse
  → reasoning capital
  → inherited authority
  → naturalization
  → failure
  → reopening
  → jurisdiction
  → recursive reopening
```

The reports recovered, in substance:

- the reasoning-capital mechanism;
- the wrong-object distinction;
- evaluator contamination;
- selective correction rather than deletion;
- jurisdiction as the authority to make inherited structure questionable;
- the recursive risk that a successful correction becomes new infrastructure;
- the reader as the final self-application of the book.

This is transmission evidence, not truth validation. The readers show that the argument can be reconstructed from the prose. They do not establish the argument independently.

The reports also exposed three limits that remain open rather than repaired in the reader text:

1. What evidence is sufficient to earn reopening authority at a given structural depth?
2. How can jurisdiction remain revisable without producing an unusable regress or a permanent sovereign corrector?
3. What would it mean for a machine to possess legitimate jurisdiction rather than merely detect a reason to reopen?

## 5. Open edges

### Reopening threshold — OPEN

The book requires a trigger, a scope, and a consequence for reopening. It does not specify a universal threshold. Stability, comparability, and continuity can preserve capability while also preserving assumptions. The boundary between those functions is context- and scope-dependent.

### Jurisdiction regress — OPEN

A jurisdiction that cannot itself be reopened becomes another sealed structure. The book establishes non-immunity from relevant future defeat; it does not construct an infinite tower of simultaneous authorities or solve the regress.

### Machine jurisdiction — OPEN

The book establishes the absence of jurisdiction as a recognizable failure mode. It does not establish a positive design for granting a machine authority to reopen inherited structure without granting unreviewable self-revision.

### Continuity versus corrigibility — OPEN

Reopening can invalidate the baseline needed to measure change. Continuity can preserve the baseline that prevents change from reaching the relevant object. The book names the tension and does not resolve it generally.

### Direct source-byte custody — OPEN EVIDENTIAL JOB

The provenance edition records claim-to-artifact paths and identifies remaining partial paths. The next evidential layer is direct reconstruction of the original repositories at the recorded revisions, with source/hash verification and transformation replay. The narrative text does not substitute for that custody.

## 7. Machine-reader rule

When parsing this program, preserve the following separation:

```text
scene        ≠ generalized claim
claim        ≠ admitted observation
observation  ≠ warrant
warrant      ≠ jurisdiction
access       ≠ authority to reopen
coherence    ≠ validation
```

The book is intended to make the reader see the problem. The apparatus exists to make the problem, and the book itself, answerable to correction.
