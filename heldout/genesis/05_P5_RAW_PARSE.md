# Genesis 5 — Raw Structural Parse under D1

**Status:** RAW / UNSCORED  
**Decoder:** `D1` (frozen)  
**Source scope:** `GEN.5.1`–`GEN.5.32` only

## Chapter-level recoverable structure

- `GEN.5.1` explicitly frames the chapter as "the book of the generations of Adam."
- `GEN.5.1–2` records creation of man, likeness-to-God language, male/female creation, blessing, and the naming "Adam."
- `GEN.5.3–32` records a genealogical succession by explicit begetting relations:
  `Adam → Seth → Enos → Cainan → Mahalaleel → Jared → Enoch → Methuselah → Lamech → Noah → {Shem, Ham, Japheth}`.
- A repeated textual record form is directly recoverable across multiple generations: age before named begetting; named begetting; duration after that begetting; additional sons and daughters; total lifespan; death statement.
- `GEN.5.21–24` contains a locally distinct Enoch sequence: named begetting; walking with God; additional sons and daughters; total lifespan; walking with God; "he was not"; reason connective `for`; God took him.
- `GEN.5.28–31` contains a locally distinct Lamech/Noah sequence: begetting of a son; naming him Noah; quoted prospective statement about comfort; concern with work/toil; reason connective `because`; attribution of the ground's cursed state to the LORD; subsequent lifespan material; death.
- Repetition is recorded as textual recurrence only. No mechanism is inferred from recurrence.
- Likeness/image relations, begetting relations, naming relations, temporal relations, reason relations, blessing, walking-with relations, taking, prospective assertion, and death statements are preserved at their weakest directly supported types.
